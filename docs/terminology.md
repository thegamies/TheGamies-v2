# Terminology

Use these terms consistently in product UI and code.

### Community

A group that can host an awards event (podcast, streamer, Discord, site, friend group).

### Voice

A verified host or designated community representative. Do not rename to admin, judge, creator, or expert in public UI.

### Community ballot

A ballot submitted by an eligible audience / community member.

### Voice ballot

A ballot submitted by a Voice.

### Combined result

Final calculation combining Community and Voice results per the event's scoring rules.

### Category

An award separate from the main ranked Game of the Year ballot (e.g. Best Multiplayer). May use single selection, multiple selection, or ranking depending on configuration.

### Official Vote

Scheduled event with opening time, deadline, and final locked result.

### Always-Live Aggregate

Ongoing community ranking derived from members' personal lists (post-v1).
